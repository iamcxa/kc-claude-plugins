Finding 1 (MINOR, hierarchy)
  cites: preview-desktop-dark-3-variables.png, preview-desktop-dark-12-variables.png
  seen: The bright white monospace variable names are at least as strong as the 13px title, and the amber 14px icon is dim. The block is plain text with no container, so it reads like part of the reply rather than a warning.

Finding 2 (MINOR, spacing)
  cites: preview-desktop-dark-12-variables.png, preview-compact-dark-12-variables.png

  author's proposed decline: Kent's repositories map a handful of variables; capping or grouping would hide names, which are the point of the row. Twelve was a stress case.

Finding 3 (MINOR, spacing)
