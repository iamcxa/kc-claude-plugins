Reviewer: kc-dev-flow-2:engineering-reviewer, opus, 2026-10-20. Read-only. Design at state HEAD 55783f69 (status ideation); package kc-dev-flow-2 0.26.0 (origin/main ad44f0cb).

Verdict: D1 is safe with conditions. D2 and D3 are safe with conditions, and those conditions must be met before the ideation gate. D6 is not mine to settle: it is a risk-acceptance call for the Captain, and the design states its reason for it as fact when the reason is unverified.

As written, the design does not keep its D2 promise that FO "cannot merge a PR the Captain did not name". The check reads the merge permission from the most recent approved validation question. That question can be one FO wrote and recorded itself, and it can belong to an earlier head (commit) than the one being merged. Both shapes already exist in this workflow's records. The fixes are small. Nothing here needs a redesign. The gate question must not say "as written".
