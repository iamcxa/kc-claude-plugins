## Problem

The kernel requires the smallest sufficient route but does not bound the accepted outcome before acceptance criteria expand. A first iteration can therefore combine the primary journey with additional actors, independently releasable surfaces, and long-tail recovery until the appetite is no longer credible. Define a portable judgment rule that makes the first accepted outcome complete for one releasable journey while allowing later iterations to cover the remaining problem space; safety, data integrity, destructive-action protection, and compatibility remain hard invariants rather than deferred coverage.
