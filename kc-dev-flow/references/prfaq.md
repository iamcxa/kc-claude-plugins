# PRFAQ

`captain_gate_presentation` is true when this stage's output goes to the Captain
for a decision. It carries the form of an artifact the stage already owes; it
adds no stage, no scope, and no obligation beyond that form.

## The work definition

The definition opens as a PRFAQ, in the work item body above its receipt: a
press release written as though the work were done, then an FAQ. The general
form is assumed; only these are added.

- At least one diagram of the change in shape or state, with prose under it.
- The Captain's own words where they exist, verbatim in their original language,
  with their date.
- Each FAQ answer in three sentences or fewer, pointing at the section that
  carries the detail. Cite an `AC-N` by its identifier and do not copy its text,
  so the extractors keep one source.
- The machine-read sections — the complete non-goal list, the work profile
  receipt, the `AC-N` bullets, the route-back conditions — keep their headings
  and their place.

## The verifying stage

A stage that verifies the work leads with its verdict, then reuses that press
release verbatim, marking each of its sentences `observed`, `not run`, or
`false`. A `false` mark carries the contradicting observation on the same line;
apart from those marks the stage adds no paragraph of its own. Its FAQ answers
evidence rather than design:

- Per `AC-N`, was the evidence run or read? A reading is not a pass.
- Which guard was watched refusing, and what stayed green beside it?
