**Real.** A current-head Codex P1 produces adapter exit 1 with `1 at P0/P1`; the record saves `outcome: findings`. Thus “the old checker blocked all unresolved P1s” would be false. Its actual rule was configured reviewer declaration/outcome citation; the workflow's materiality, P1 block and Captain-waiver policy remained human-owned.

Relevant code: `delivery_gate.py:793-806`, `1297-1311`, `1339-1378`; workflow FO steps: review findings 1–5.

The four review-disposition fields are: released workflow = public-PR record preparation; observable harm = an undecided P1/High review can reach an apparently passing merge-question preflight; protected boundary = `contract[kc-dev-flow-2/references/sd/workflow.md#fo-steps-review-findings]`, reviewer P1/Material blocks until repaired/revalidated or Captain-waived; trigger = the two adapter-backed `0/0` reproductions above. This is a safety gap, not evidence that a real PR was merged.

## Reported finding: Copilot quota refusal

**Real.** Actual adapter exit 5 yields `not-run`, no eligible evidence identity and no extra review round. `quota-refusal-no-unread-or-retry` has no Copilot unread decision or retry in its question; record `check/question=0/0`. The corresponding legacy refusal line and saved output, with retry omitted, produce `1/1`. The disclosed control produces `0/0` in both routes.

The old check was conditional on supplied text: it did not independently discover every omitted refusal. Once a refusal line was supplied, it checked quote/id/source and the retry; a configured second reviewer also needed an outcome. The new contract deliberately removes copied refusal-quote boilerplate, but **retains** the unread decision and quota-restoration retry in FO's question (Readers and limits). Those retained duties are not enforced by `facts_question` or the observed writer. Requiring the old copied quote again would undo the approved removal rather than fix this reader gap.

The four fields are: released workflow = listed Copilot returns a non-review answer on the current head; observable harm = Captain receives a question omitting the required unread/retry choice; protected boundary = `contract[kc-dev-flow-2/references/delivery-record.md#readers-and-limits]`, unread reviewer and retry belong in the question, with no automatic re-request; trigger = adapter exit 5 and record `0/0` in the omission case. No retry/request was sent by this probe.

## Other differences found by the full audit

- Missing, duplicate or wrong conditional-reviewer declarations and empty trigger reasons: old `1/1`, record `0/0`. Automatically observed outcome identities do not answer the unobservable trigger judgment.
- Single- or double-quoted `profile: prod`, with a valid security report and a pending second reviewer: old `1/1`, record `0/0` on `Second reviewer: not triggered`. Plain prod and db still hold. The record branch duplicates the trigger guard with a raw regex instead of the existing frontmatter parser.
- Empty, nonhex and 41-hex `--match-head-commit` values: old `check/question=0/1`, record `0/0`. An empty parsed list passes `any(...)`; overlong tokens can be partially matched. A different valid head is refused, and a valid prefix passes in both routes.
- Reviewer configuration made invalid after a passing check: old question 2, record question 0. This is a demonstrated reader-parity gap, not a claim that a real workflow changed configuration mid-ask.
- `Unchecked counts: 1` and the existing-item scope mark are generated by the old route and omitted by the record route, with no corresponding record fields. ADRs 0072 and 0082 still describe those automatic disclosures. Earlier reports noted their applicability as unverified; the audit now exercises the loss. Do not silently restore question mutation against the immutable-question design: FO must resolve this boundary, routing a Captain decision if the accepted behavior changes.

## Smallest repair proposed, not applied

The unapplied, context-check-only `proposal-not-applied.patch` stays in the nine-file boundary: `scripts/delivery_gate.py` **+34/-4**, `references/delivery-record.md` **+9/-0**; total **+43/-4**, measured by `git apply --numstat`. It was never applied or executed. The production candidate delta remains **+0/-0**. Regression tests for this proposed behavior are not authored/applied; their future line delta is not measured.

Use one record-reader helper for FO judgments and unread/retry decisions, called by both record commands. Reuse `reviewer_problems` for configured declarations and quoted-profile triggers, reread current configuration at question time and compare its reviewer names with the saved observations. Require a nonempty bounded merge-head token before comparison. Existing `reviewer`, `head`, `exit`, `outcome`, `evidence` and `question_sha256` fields suffice for these checks; no new factual field is needed.

The proposed `Review decision` means an FO judgment tied to one review. `findings` needs an explicit disposition; `answered` needs an explicit clean/disposed judgment after FO reads it. Human decisions stay in the question and are bound by its hash; they are not copied into the factual record. Copilot `not-run` needs an unread decision and conditional retry, without copied quote/source prose. The contract needs these **reader rules**, not a new adapter outcome grammar.

Important limit: this proposal requires explicit FO judgment, not proof of each finding's truthful classification, repair or Captain waiver. The old route never provided that stronger automatic guarantee. If FO wants automatic per-finding P1/waiver verification, the current adapter/record contract lacks sufficient structured severity/disposition/waiver evidence; that is a separate contract/scope decision, not a property I can claim for this minimal proposal.

The two computed disclosure losses are deliberately excluded from that illustrative patch pending the authority decision above. No new files, versions, workflow budget change, adapter edit or outside consumer repair is proposed. Future validation must turn the omission cases into desired holds, retain clean/disposed/untriggered/not-run controls, and rerun the appropriate suites after an authorized repair.
