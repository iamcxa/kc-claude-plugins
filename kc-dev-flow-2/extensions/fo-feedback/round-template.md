# Feedback round <number>

- Identity, status (`open`, `closed-full`, `closed-partial`, `cancelled`), opened/closed dates, interviewer and workflow; prior ownership ended if replaced.
- Trigger and cadence/question grant; sourced accepted batch membership/completion, distinct completions since anchor, anchor before/after. Early reset or partial-anchor movement needs its authority; cancellation leaves the anchor unchanged.
- Package commit/version, extension inventory and applicable core topics; frozen roster with confirmed identities/areas, source/date and transport.

## Coverage

One row per FO and extension/core topic: enabled, disabled or unknown; answered, pending, deferred or unavailable. Keep unavailable respondents and missing answers visible. No per-rule rows. Newly discovered FOs/topics are explicit additions or next-round entries. Unobserved events do not establish compliance.

## Exchanges

Use round + confirmed FO + topic as the key. Copy the four versioned questions from EXTENSION.md into one bundled message per enabled topic; record specialist additions and any fixed question not asked. Record pending intent, returned send reference, answer source/date and original words or labelled condensation. Reconcile uncertain sends before another send; duplicate callbacks update the same entry. Preserve missing historical identities and sources.

## Evidence comparison

For each claim: dated event/task, operator statement, checked source/revision, independent observer verdict/reference, ruling-ledger/original-ruling reference, uncertainty and disagreement. Unavailable originals remain unverified; predictions are labelled. Useful events are capability candidates, not README claims.

## Findings and close

Search existing tasks by outcome; link a match, otherwise record a bounded backlog proposal with affected rule, evidence, improvement, tradeoff and disposition. Do not approve or implement it here. Close with answered/missing entries, explicit status and next anchor under the cadence grant. Append corrections while preserving earlier answers. The index links rounds, at most one open round and the current anchor; claims live in their round once.
