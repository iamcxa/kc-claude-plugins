Slack notice, proposed (DISPOSABLE mock drawn locally; Slack draws the real one from the Block Kit payload)

One notice per accepted event and outcome. A resent copy of an accepted event (same source and id) posts nothing; after NOT started the claim is released, so the sender's retry is accepted and posts its own notice. The first event of an incident (same source and subject) says "New incident" and starts the agent; a later one says "Joins incident" and starts no agent. NOT started has a red bar, UNCERTAIN an orange one. The read-back self-test is a fifth kind, posted only by the setup read-back. Event text is escaped: "<!channel>" below is plain text, not a mention. Field text is cut at 200 characters (the last light notice).

Light theme, desktop width: one day's channel in order (new incident, its later event, another incident), then NOT started and the sender's retry that started it, UNCERTAIN, read-back self-test, escaped mention, field text cut at 200
