- Suggested direction (the Chief Engineer's, not a ruling): reuse v1's eight rules for how to write, and add only what they lack. (1) The root `docs/architecture.md` is a map, a table from question to owning document; a small project keeps one `docs/architecture.md` on the skeleton and splits when a second flow arrives. (2) A topic document states what is the case today on a fixed skeleton: what it covers and leaves to others, Rules, proven and not proven, known limits, governing ADRs; other sections are free and titled as the reader's question. (3) Rules and numbers are stated in the architecture document; a link to an ADR does not stand in for the sentence. (4) An ADR records a ruling a person made, in their words; when one replaces another, the older one's Status line changes and nothing else. (5) A new topic document gets a map row; an idea for later goes to the story map or the task, never the architecture or an ADR. The smallest package edits that would carry this: a sixth home in `read-adr`'s list, the principles phrase narrowed to a person's ruling, and `retained-documents.md` replaced by these lines with a pointer to the eight rules. The exact text trialled is `CONVENTION2` in `poc/harness/world.py`.
